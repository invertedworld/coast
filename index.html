An island that never ends to the east or west, between half a kilometre and two kilometres wide. Villages are laid out from the rules of medieval hill towns, with open country, olive groves and a coastal railway between them. Draw distance adjusts itself to hold 60 frames per second: —.

The cats are from Michi-Neko: The Cat Philosopher’s Path by c1t1zen1, used under the MIT licence.

Train and ferry

RRide in front of the passenger train. R or V again steps off beside the track
CFollow the train being ridden, or else the nearer one, from behind. The mouse moves the camera round it and +− change the distance. C or V again steps off beside the track
GRide in front of the goods train. G or V again steps off beside the track
FBoard the nearest ferry wherever it is. While it lies at a landing it may also be boarded by its gangway. On board, W A S D walk about the decks and the mouse looks round. F or V again steps ashore, or at the next landing while the ferry is under way

Buildings

Walk into a doorHouses, barns, churches, towers, the station, the keeper’s cottage and the ferry’s saloon and wheelhouse may be entered. A door swings open when it is walked into, from either side, and closes again behind

Controller

The left stick moves and the right stick looks. A jumps, B switches the view, X boards the ferry, Y rides with the train and LB follows it. RB sets tilt-shift, the triggers zoom, pressing the left stick switches between running and walking, the D-pad turns and tilts, Back mutes and Start shows this panel
